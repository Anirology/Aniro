Privacy policy

Aniro is a private, single-user productivity dashboard. This page explains the information it uses, especially when Google Calendar is connected.

Information stored in Aniro

Aniro stores the plans, goals, tasks, habits, work sessions, budget and expense records you enter, along with settings needed to run the app. The hosted app stores these records in a Neon Postgres database connected to Vercel. App access is limited through Vercel Authentication.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Aniro asks to read your calendar list and to view and manage events on calendars you own. It reads events to show your commitments alongside your plan. It creates, updates, or deletes Google events only when you use the relevant controls in Aniro. Google authorization tokens are encrypted before storage. You can disconnect Google Calendar from the Plan page; this removes Aniro's stored token. Events already created in Google Calendar remain unless you explicitly delete them.

AI assistance

If you send a message to Aniru, the app sends your message and limited planning context to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. The app can include task or goal titles and finance totals if you enable those sharing options. It does not send individual expense records by default. Aniru does not change your records on its own.

Providers and sharing

Vercel hosts the app, Neon stores its database, and Google provides Calendar and optional Gemini services. Information is shared with these providers only as needed for those features. Aniro does not sell personal data or use advertising trackers on this information site.

Your choices

You can leave Google Calendar disconnected, disconnect it later, or export your Aniro data in the app. To request deletion of hosted records or ask a privacy question, email aniruththens@gmail.com. Provider backups may remain for a limited period under their retention policies.

Google API data is used only to provide the Calendar features described above.
